# Community conduct

Welcome contributors of every background. Be respectful, constructive and patient.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and unwanted contact are unacceptable.
Review ideas and code, not people's worth. Respect privacy and consent.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, pause discussions, or restrict
participation. Report concerns privately to the repository owner where possible.
Reports will be reviewed with care and discretion. This policy applies in project
spaces and when representing the project in public.
